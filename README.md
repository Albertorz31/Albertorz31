## 🚀 Featured Projects

### ABASYS

**Todo lo que mueve tu operación — by Apollonix**

A modular multi-tenant SaaS platform designed to centralize and manage business operations.

The platform is being designed around independent modules that organizations can enable according to their operational needs, including inventory, procurement, food operations and user administration.

**Tech Stack**

`TypeScript` · `NestJS` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma` · `Vue 3` · `Docker`

**Key concepts**

`Multi-tenancy` · `Modular Architecture` · `Authentication` · `Authorization` · `Role & Permission Management`

> 🚧 Currently in active development.

### Wayra Operations Platform

Full-stack operational management system developed for the hospitality industry.

The platform centralizes several internal business processes including inventory, suppliers, food services, accommodation, laundry, equipment maintenance and operational reporting.

**Tech Stack**

`Vue 3` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `MongoDB` · `Docker` · `Nginx` · `DigitalOcean`
